# V0 — straight exterior stair

Same floor plans as the L-stair design, but with a **straight** exterior stair
along the south wall (the original approach before folding it into an L).

## Stair

- 19 risers @ 7.37″, 18 treads @ 10.5″, 36″ wide
- 4′×4′ top landing at the SE entry door
- Straight run 15′-9″ along the south wall
- **Yard intrusion:** ~9′–10′ past the southwest corner into the open yard
  (this is why the L-stair and spiral variants exist)
