# ADU — upstairs studio with kitchenette + full bath

Second-floor **studio ADU** over the lean first-floor bedroom addition.
Spiral access; **both stories flush at the 5′ east setback** (no 9′ upper-story
inset, no cantilever).

> **Confirm with Richmond Planning** that ADU standards apply and that the RL2
> 9′ above-ground side yard does not apply to this unit. Design assumes flush
> 5′ side walls under ADU rules (state ADU law often allows as little as 4′).
