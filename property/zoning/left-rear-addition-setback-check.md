| Standard | Required | Measured / proposed | Status |

| Side yard — ground floor | **5.0′ (60″)** | **4.375′ (52.5″)** | **Short by 7.5″** |

## Can you stay flush?

**Still not as-of-right**, but much closer than the OSM map suggested.

- Flush **ground floor** would be only **7.5″** into the required 5′ side yard.
- Flush **second story** would still miss the required **9′** side yard by ~4.6′.
- RMC 15.04.606.030.B: residential enlargements must meet current standards.
- Possible discretionary relief: AUP under 15.04.606.070.C.2 and/or variance — confirm with Richmond Planning.

## Conforming alternative

- Ground floor: pull east wall in by **7.5″** (to the 5′ line).
- Second story: further inset to the **9′** line (~4.6′ in from the existing east wall), or keep the whole 2-story mass at 9′.
